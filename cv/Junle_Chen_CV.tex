\section*{Research Profile}

PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Professor Xiaofang Zhou. My research focuses on interaction and context management for LLM agents, enabling them to maintain user intent and task goals across multi-turn, long-horizon interactions.

\section*{Research Interests}

Interaction and Context Management for LLM Agents, with emphasis on:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{User Interaction}: Tracking user intent and preference changes in multi-turn dialogue.
\item \textbf{Environment Interaction}: Maintaining user intent and task goals throughout long-horizon execution.
\end{itemize}
